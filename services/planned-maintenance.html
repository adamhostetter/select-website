Planned Maintenance

Programmatic service plans built around your facility — predictable scheduling, asset-level tracking, and reporting your CFO can take to a budget meeting.

What's included

A maintenance program tailored to your equipment.

Every planned maintenance contract is built around your facility's equipment mix, operating hours, and risk tolerance — not a one-size template.

Scheduled inspections

Quarterly, semi-annual, or seasonal visits aligned to manufacturer recommendations and how hard your equipment actually runs.

Filter & belt changes

Filters, belts, lubrication, coil cleaning, refrigerant checks, and combustion analysis — all tracked and reported per visit.

Asset-level reporting

Every piece of equipment gets a record — what we found, what we fixed, what's coming due. Reporting you can take to your CFO.

Combustion & refrigerant analysis

Burner tuning, refrigerant pressures, superheat/subcool measurements taken and logged — early warning on performance drift.

24/7 emergency response

PM customers get priority dispatch with a guaranteed response window — your equipment doesn't fail on a schedule, neither do we.

Capital planning

Annual reviews of equipment condition with replacement recommendations and rough-order budget numbers for your planning cycle.

Why it matters

Maintained equipment lasts longer, costs less, breaks less.

Reactive service is expensive. Emergency overtime, expedited parts, downtime in the building — every avoidable failure is a multiple of what a quarterly PM visit would have cost.

A well-run planned maintenance program extends equipment life 20-30%, cuts energy consumption, and turns unpredictable repair spend into a predictable line item.

  • Fewer emergency callouts and after-hours surprises
  • Lower energy bills from properly tuned equipment
  • Longer equipment life — defer capital replacement
  • Warranty compliance with documented service records
  • Better indoor air quality and tenant comfort
  • Predictable annual maintenance budget
